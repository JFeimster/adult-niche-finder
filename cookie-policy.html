Cookie Policy

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by a browser. Local storage is a related browser capability that stores key-value data.

Cookies used by this Service

The Adult Niche Finder experience does not currently use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking cookies. No third-party cookies are set by default.

Local storage

The Save My Results feature may write saved persona summaries to local storage. This is under your control and can be cleared via the app's Clear Saved Data action or your browser settings.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings at any time. Blocking local storage may remove saved results functionality.

Any future addition of cookies will be reflected on this page.
