Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: November 4, 2026

What is Adult Niche Finder?

About the assessment

Adult Niche Finder is an interactive creator identity assessment for adults exploring creator opportunities. It maps personality, preferences, boundaries, and goals into one of ten creator archetype lanes.

Is this a scientific personality test?

Fit estimates

No. Match scores and fit estimates are exploratory heuristic estimates produced by a weighted decision engine. They are not scientific measurements and are not predictions of success, income, or suitability.

What happens to my quiz answers?

Privacy

Quiz answers are kept in browser memory during the session and are not transmitted. Saved results use local storage only and can be cleared at any time.

How do hard boundaries change the result?

Boundaries

Anonymity and no-live preferences are treated as binding constraints. Live-only strategies are not recommended when live interaction is declined, and collaboration branding requires explicit affirmative interest.

How are alternative matches chosen?

Alternatives

Alternatives are the next strongest archetypes after the primary reveal, using the same deterministic scoring and tie-breaking rules.

Is the content on this site explicit?

Content policy

The experience is designed to remain non-explicit and avoids explicit sexual descriptions or minor-appropriation concerns. See the DMCA / NSFW Policy for details.

Which platforms are recommended?

Platforms

Recommendations cover subscription, live, social discovery, community/membership, and personal website categories. Platform eligibility, identity verification, and content policies must be verified by the creator.

How do I share or save my result?

Result actions

You can save results locally, copy your niche brief, download the playbook, or share your persona via the Web Share API with a `?persona=` deep link.
