The Method

How the Lane Mapping Works

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The short version

You answered a series of preference and boundary questions. The engine weighted each answer toward the ten creator archetypes, applied your hard boundaries, and ranked the strongest lane.

The question sequence

The assessment walks through ten dimensions:

  • Motivation — why you want to create.
  • Personality — how your persona comes across.
  • Visual identity — aesthetic lanes.
  • Camera comfort — face visibility and anonymity.
  • Formats — preferred content formats.
  • Engagement — how you want audiences to feel.
  • Boundaries — privacy, live interaction, visibility, collaboration.
  • Strengths — where you already shine.
  • Collaboration — solo vs. consensual collaboration.
  • Capacity — where you are starting from.

The weighted engine

Answers map to one or more archetypes with configurable weights. Scores accumulate across questions. The leading archetype becomes your reveal, with two alternatives.

Fit estimates

Fit scores are exploratory heuristic estimates produced from accumulated archetype weights. They are not scientific measurements and are not predictions of success or income.

Hard boundaries

Anonymity and no-live preferences are treated as binding. Live-only strategies are never recommended when live interaction is declined, and collaboration branding requires explicit affirmative interest.

Alternatives

The two alternative matches are the next strongest archetypes after the reveal, using the same deterministic scoring and tie-breaking rules.

Adult Niche Finder is a creative exploration tool. Match scores are exploratory heuristic estimates.
