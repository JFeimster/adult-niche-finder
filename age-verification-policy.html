Adult Niche Finder is intended for adults aged 18 and older who are exploring adult-oriented creator opportunities. Content is not directed to minors.

The assessment begins with an age confirmation step. This confirmation is a lightweight front-end gate and is not identity verification, and no birth date or identity document is collected through the Service.

Proper age-verification services may be integrated in the future if required. Until then, the Service relies on the front-end confirmation and responsible content framing.
