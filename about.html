Our story

Adult Niche Finder began inside a neon-lit casting room — part nightclub, part audition stage, part editorial studio. The idea: before the internet gives you a name, a niche, and a strategy, you should know who you are.

What happens here

You answer a short, private assessment. The engine maps your motivations, personality, boundaries, and ambitions onto one of ten creator archetypes. Then you get a persona reveal, a full playbook, and a next move.

What it is not

The Studio is a creative exploration tool, not a scientific lab, not a financial advisor, and not a content marketplace. Matches are exploratory estimates. Strategy output is a starting point for your own creative work.
