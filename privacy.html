Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Overview

Adult Niche Finder ("the Service") is a creative exploration tool that helps adults discover potential creator identity lanes. This policy explains what information the Service handles and how it is treated.

Assessment answers

Quiz responses are used in real time to compute a persona match and are kept in browser memory for the duration of the session. They are not transmitted to any server, and no account is required.

Saved results

If you choose the Save My Results action, a public-safe archetype summary is written to your browser's local storage under the key adult-niche-finder:saved-result. You may remove it at any time with Clear Saved Data.

Analytics and tracking

The Service uses no third-party analytics or tracking by default. See the Cookie Policy for how local storage and any future cookie use are handled.

Personal information

The Service does not require or collect personally identifiable information, legal names, or identity documents. No birth dates are collected.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes and contact

Policy changes will be reflected on this page. For questions, contact the operator through the project's public repository or support channel.
